Exporting your leads from Ricochet360

A quick walkthrough for exporting your leads from Ricochet360.
STEPS

Export your leads in 5 steps

1

Open the Leads tab

Log into your Ricochet360 portal. In the left sidebar, click Leads (or Search Leads) to open your leads list.

2

Set your timeframe

At the top of your leads dashboard, use the date filter to set the window your account manager asked for — for example Past 30 Days, Past Month, or a custom range. You don't need to narrow it down to GOAL's campaigns: pull the complete export for that timeframe and we'll scrub out everything else on our side.

3

Select all the records

Once the filtered list appears, check the master checkbox in the top-left header of the list to select every record at once.

4

Export to a CSV file

In the action menu (or table tools), click Export / Generate CSV. Keep the default “All Fields” so Phone Number, Lead Name, and Status/Disposition are all included. Save it to your computer as a .csv file.

5

Email the file to me

Reply to your account manager's email with the .csv attached. Important: don't modify, clean, or rewrite anything — especially the phone numbers. They're how we tie your results back, so send the file exactly as it downloads.
