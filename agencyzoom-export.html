How to export your leads from AgencyZoom

A short, five-step export your Account Manager will occasionally ask for — usually before a performance review. It takes about five minutes.
5 steps
~5 minutes
Export as CSV
Why we ask for this

Your export lets our team match the prospects you have quoted and bound back to the campaigns that generated them. That is how we measure what is actually working — and steer your budget toward the leads and lines that turn into real, written policies. Your data is handled securely and only your campaign records are used.

THE EXPORT

Five steps, start to finish

Follow these in order. Each step below shows roughly where to look on screen.

1

Open your Leads section

Log in to your AgencyZoom account. In the left-hand navigation menu, click Leads to view your active opportunities.

2

Switch to List view

By default AgencyZoom shows your records as a visual card layout — the Pipeline view. Near the top-center you will see the blue Pipeline toggle. Click Leads right next to it to switch into the tabular List view.

3

Apply the timeframe filter

Click the date range filter on the screen and select the range your Account Manager specified — for example Past 30 Days, Past Month, or a custom range.

Don't filter out other vendors or channels. Export the whole list for the period. Our team securely filters down to just the records that match your campaign.
4

Export to CSV

Open the Actions dropdown in the top-right corner, then choose Export (or Export Leads). Make sure the format is set to CSV (.csv), download it, and save it somewhere you can find it.

5

Send the file to your Account Manager

Attach the downloaded .csv file to your email reply and send it over. From there, our team handles the rest.

Prefer your shared client portal? Uploading the file there works too — whichever is easier for you.

Before you send: don't reformat the file

Please don't change or reformat any phone numbers or names in the file. Our matching system uses the exact phone numbers as the primary key to line your records up with our campaign data — editing them can break the import. Just export and send it as-is.

What happens next

Once we have your file, we securely match it to your campaign, update your reporting with what quoted and bound, and use it to steer budget toward what is closing. Nothing more is needed from you — and if anything looks off, just reply and we will walk you through it.
